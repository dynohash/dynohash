# Security

## Report a problem

Email **security@dynohash.com**.

Please do not open a public issue for a security problem.

Plain text is best. Tell us:

- what you found,
- the steps to see it again,
- your DynoHash version (`dynohash --version`, or About in the app).

We aim to reply within 7 days. We will say what we found and what we will do.
We credit you when the fix ships, if you want that. We cannot pay bounties.

## In scope

- Official DynoHash builds: the files in this repo's releases.
- Release checksums (`SHA256SUMS`) and signatures (`.minisig`).
- The website, `dynohash.com`, and the stats server, `api.dynohash.com`.
- Fake sites, fake builds and fake accounts that pretend to be DynoHash. Send those too.

Only the latest release gets fixes.

## Ground rules

- Test with your own data and your own rig.
- No denial-of-service tests, no spam, no tricking people.
- Give us a fair time to fix it before you publish it.

Good-faith research that follows these rules is welcome. We will not take action against it.

## What we never ask for

- Your seed phrase or private keys.
- Your passwords: pool, exchange or email.
- Remote access to your computer.
- Money. DynoHash is free. There is no "pro", "unlocked" or "0% fee" build.
- Running a script someone pasted into a chat.

We only write from support@dynohash.com or security@dynohash.com, and only after you wrote to us or signed up for the beta.

## Official sources

- Downloads: **dynohash.com** and **github.com/dynohash** only.
- Every file has a SHA-256 checksum and a minisign signature. [How to check them](README.md#verify-your-download)
- [How to spot a fake](https://dynohash.com/spot-a-fake/)

## Ran something you are not sure about?

Use a different, clean device. From there, move your coins and change the passwords for your pool and exchanges.
Then tell us: security@dynohash.com
